Cookies.txt Converter

Turn a browser cookie export into a yt-dlp-ready Netscape cookies.txt.

Cookies are as sensitive as your password. Conversion happens entirely in this page — nothing is uploaded. Never share a cookies.txt; anyone with it can use your logged-in session.
This page can't read your cookies for you — that needs a browser extension. Export them first with a trusted extension like “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY” (it can output JSON), then paste that here. Pairs with the YouTube MP3 Studio.
