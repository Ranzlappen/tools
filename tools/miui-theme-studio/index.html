How to apply on your phone
  1. Build and download the .mtz.
  2. Copy it to your phone (USB, cloud, or download it directly on the device).
  3. Open the File Manager, find the .mtz, and tap it — it opens in the Themes app.
  4. Alternatively: Themes app → profile/account → Import (or the three-dot menu) → pick the .mtz.
  5. Tap Apply. You may need to confirm a reboot for fonts / boot animation.

Themes are unsigned. If applying is blocked, your region/build may restrict third-party themes — that's a device policy, not a broken file.
